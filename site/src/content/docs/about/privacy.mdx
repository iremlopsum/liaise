This site counts how its pages and its intro are used, so the docs can be improved. It uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

## What a page view records

- The page, any `ref` or `utm_*` tag in its address, and the site that linked to it (only that site's address, as browsers send it).
- Your timezone, used to guess a country; your browser's language; and your browser, operating system and device as categories (for example "Safari", "macOS", "desktop").
- How long the page was in front of you and in use, and whether you left the site from it.
- On the docs: what you searched for and whether a result was picked, which Copy buttons worked, and links to GitHub and npm.
- In the playground and the intro: which example you ran, how far the intro got, and how the challenge's checks went.

## What it never records

Your IP address, your exact screen size, your User-Agent string, or anything that could tell this visit apart from your next one. Each page view gets a random number that exists only while the page is open.

## Turning it off

If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, nothing is recorded. Automated browsers are never recorded either.

## Where it goes, and for how long

Records go to Google's Firebase, stored in the EU. Each one is kept for 30 days, then only daily totals remain. Only the site's owner can read them.
